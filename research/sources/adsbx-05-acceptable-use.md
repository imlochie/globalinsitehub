# ADS-B Exchange official Acceptable Use Policy

**URL:** https://www.adsbexchange.com/acceptable-use-policy/  
**Checked:** 2026-09-30  
**Evidence type:** direct retrieval of official policy

The policy returned HTTP 200. It defines the applicable agreement as the linked ADS-B Exchange/JETNET Terms of Use or a superseding written agreement. Its use restrictions prohibit publishing, reselling, transmitting, broadcasting, or distributing the Services or data acquired from them unless the company authorizes it in advance in writing. It also restricts sharing credentials and exceeding documented access limits.
