**Checked:** 2026-09-30  
**Evidence type:** official license summary and full text

The ODbL summary describes the rights to share, create, and adapt a database, subject to attribution, share-alike, and keeping the database open. The full license is controlling; it distinguishes database rights from rights in individual contents and includes specific terms for public use and conveyance of derivative databases and produced works. This is a legal license, not a provider-specific confirmation of Signalwatch’s proposed use.
